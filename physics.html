🎯 Course Objectives & Program Outcomes (POs)

Course Objectives (COs)

  • Develop a strong conceptual foundation in static and dynamic electromagnetic fields[cite: 1, 2].
  • Understand wave phenomena, optical behavior, and modern quantum physical principles.
  • Analyze atomic structures, nuclear energy transitions, and semiconductor electronics.
  • Apply physical equations to real-world engineering and technological applications.

Program Outcomes (POs) Covered

  • PO1 (Engineering Knowledge): Apply fundamental principles of physics to solve complex technical problems.
  • PO2 (Problem Analysis): Formulate and evaluate physical systems using analytical tools and vector calculus[cite: 2].
  • PO3 (Modern Tool Usage): Visualize field patterns and semiconductor behavior using digital models[cite: 1, 2].
  • PO4 (Lifelong Learning): Build self-paced inquiry skills for modern technological advancements.
